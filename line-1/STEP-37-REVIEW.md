# Step 37 — The Measured Grains

Timing Lens advances the unchanged goal of bounded public HTTP diagnosis. It
compares a saved probe's total latency to a caller's explicit budget and
explains the measured connection-through-headers intervals, unattributed
remainder and wrapper overhead. It never assigns the remainder to body time
or calls the header interval server processing. Missing and inconsistent
measurements remain explicit. Its README gives an offline command on retained
real evidence, exact exit meanings, dependencies and limitations.

Fourteen behavioral tests passed. A single anonymous real public GET, bounded
by an eight-second watchdog, five-second worker timeout, 262,144-byte limit and
no redirects, returned 200 and valid JSON. Its probe total was 183.144 ms,
within the demonstration's 500 ms budget. This is one historical observation,
not a continuing availability or root-cause claim. No coin was needed.

The built-in image generator supplied an isolated motif: ochre grains falling
into a charcoal bowl holding a red pebble. The final prompt was: “Isolated
prehistoric pigment drawing, centered on plain pure white: a broad shallow
crescent charcoal bowl with a small red ochre pebble resting inside, three
ochre grains vertically falling above it. Worn handmade dry pigment, simple
uneven Lascaux brush marks. The whole symbol occupies the central half of the
image in both dimensions; ample white margin all around. Flat earth pigment
illustration. No text, numerals, letters, signatures, watermark, frames, hands,
people or extra objects.” White was removed and only pigment composited over
bare rock. The intermediate image was deleted. The final full wall and its
JPEG were opened for local visual review.

The full wall is a 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG at both artifacts/line-1/wall-v17.png
and continuation wall.png. The 512 × 512 JPEG is dist/line-1/37.jpg, 75,062
bytes. Record 37 names Timing Lens and links to the full PNG's SHA-256.
Only 2,013 pixels changed in x=1050..1132, y=810..905; zero changed outside
the designated bare-rock addition rectangle. Earlier paintings, frogs, rock,
cracks, light and framing remain intact. The new motif is small and subtle
in the miniature. No text, numbers or prohibited symbols were observed.

Visual limitation: no hands or handprints were added. Inherited simplified
frog hands do not resolve individual digits, so five-digit anatomy cannot be
certified without redrawing ancestors, which the assignment forbids. This is
recorded as an unmet verification requirement, not silently claimed as passed.
Local review is not independent visual approval.
